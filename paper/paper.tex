\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
A Random Forest classifier is explained with held-out permutation importance scored by ROC-AUC, matching the model's evaluation metric.
\end{abstract}
\section{Method}
The model uses 450 trees and a stratified 75/25 split. Each feature is shuffled 16 times on held-out data. Importance is the resulting decrease in ROC-AUC.
\section{Results}
Held-out ROC-AUC is 0.9945. The largest mean permutation effects include worst area, worst concave points, and worst perimeter.
\section{Interpretation}
Permutation importance measures model dependence, not causality. Correlated features can substitute for one another.
