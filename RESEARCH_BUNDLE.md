# Research Bundle Evidence Contract

## Identity
**Area:** AI Engineering  
**Study:** global explanation stability and protected-feature governance  
**Dataset:** UCI Adult (2)

## Empirical evidence required
- UCI dataset ID and DOI;
- exact target normalization;
- train/test seed and sizes;
- missing-data handling;
- all-feature and protected-excluded feature sets;
- held-out ROC-AUC for both conditions;
- permutation scoring metric and repeat count;
- per-feature mean and standard deviation of importance;
- software environment.

## Non-claims
A protected-feature exclusion experiment is not proof of fairness. Permutation importance is not causal attribution. Historical income classification is not a legitimate deployment objective by default.
